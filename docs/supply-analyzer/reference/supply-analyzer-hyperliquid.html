▸ How the numbers are made

Measured today: 437.5M HYPE staked (Hyperliquid validators, exit takes 7 days), 47.6M burned (the Assistance Fund's balance), buyback $145M in Q3 (DefiLlama). Estimated: the 207.8M staked on the Hyper Foundation's validators is taken to come from the reserve; team tranches of 9.92M a month (trackers differ on the day and the amount); staking rewards of 0.79M a month at today's rate; buybacks at Q3's pace and today's price, all of them burned. Staking in and out is not measured, so it is held flat. The pie shows the tokens in play; the reserve is the part of the max supply not paid out yet, and rewards come out of it. Each dot in the tank is the same amount of tokens. The sellable pile is the free tokens plus the staking that can leave at any time. The 1 to 10 number is 6 plus the yearly change of the pile in steps of 2.5%, between 1 and 10. A measured supply statistic, not financial advice and not a forecast. It places no orders.
