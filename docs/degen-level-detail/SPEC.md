## Why

**The row.** Today a row can carry several small flag icons, each with its own tooltip (Micro, Thin liquidity, Cliff, Revenue bought with emissions, …). A degen has to hover and read them one by one, and does not. **One number per row instead: the Degen level (1–10), as a mini version of the Degen panel, in a column of its own that you can sort by.** Every check runs in the background; nobody has to click anything.

**The chart.** Cymetica now has a Chart Analyzer: it reads a coin's price and volume chart, finds chart patterns on it and scores the chart from 1 (very bearish) to 100 (very bullish). Every coin it can read gets a small price line in the row and a bigger one in the detail, in the colour of that score, with a link that opens the coin in the analyzer. It is a reading of the chart, not advice: the analyzer's own test on data it had not learned from found that high scores were not followed by rises more often, and the screen says so.

**The detail.** It is a long stack, and the most useful pieces (Monthly revenue, Trajectory, "is the revenue real") are hard to find. New order, few words, big type, and **one table that shows which checks light up and which do not.**

**The report.** The existing Degen report button stays, but moves to the very bottom of the detail. It does two things: it fills in the four checks that data alone cannot answer (team, audits, track record, token mechanics), and it shows a short write-up that reads at a glance: a calm card with the verdict, what it is, the good, the shady and the bet, and no table.

The Degen level is the one place where several checks become one number. In Part 3 "no composite score" is about the Sectors themes only. Here the level is **display only: it is never the table's own order, a filter, a gate, or an input to Top Picks, Degen picks or any fund; the one thing it does is sort the table when a visitor clicks the Degen header.** The Chart score is display only in the same way and does not sort at all. Higher means riskier, so a coin can have a high Degen level and still be a Degen pick: the level does not replace the Degen gates and does not change them.

### B. The Degen level (computed in the background)

Whole number 1–10: **how much of a casino a coin is.** Higher = riskier. It is not a score for attractiveness; Upside and Strength stay that. Three bands with one plain sentence each: **1–3 Blue chip** "Boring on purpose." · **4–6 Spicy** "Size it small." · **7–10 Casino** "Only money you can lose."

It is built from **18 checks in six areas.** Each check is green, light blue, red, or **not checked**. The backend runs all of them on every refresh (a visitor never triggers one, except the report). Thresholds below are our first proposal; calibrate them on your top ~30 apps and write down what you settle on.

| Area (weight) | Check | Measures | Green | Light blue | Red | Data |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Control** (2.5) | Admin key | who can change the rules | Safe: 3+ signatures and at least half the owners | Safe: 2 signatures, or under half the owners | EOA, or 1 signature | contract scan |
| | Timelock | delay before a change lands | 48 h or more | 24 to under 48 h | under 24 h, or none | contract scan |
| | Admin powers | mint, pause, upgrade | none, or gated | one | two or more | contract scan |
| **Supply** (2) | Hidden supply | tokens that can leave staking or locks on the holder's own request ÷ float | under 0.25× | 0.25–1× | over 1× | holders scan |
| | Emission rate | weekly emissions ÷ float | under 4% | 4–10% | over 10% | emission data |
| | Insider flow | insider wallets to DEX or CEX, 30 d | under 0.5% of float | 0.5–1.5% | over 1.5% | holders scan |
| | Unlocks | scheduled unlocks, next 90 d, and how many tokens are already out | under 3%, and 25% of tokens or more out | 3–8%, or under 25% of tokens out | over 8%, a cliff, or under 10% of tokens out | **live today** |
| **Earnings** (2) | Real yield | revenue ÷ next-30-day emissions **at today's price** | 2× or more | 1 to under 2× | under 1× | **live today** (re-priced) |
| | Trend | shape of the last 12 months | steady or accelerating | ignition, slowing, no clear trend, or a 2.5–4.5× spike | declining, post-crash, or a spike over 4.5× | **live today** |
| | Reaches you | does a buyer of this token get the revenue | directly | after a stake or lock | haircut, wrapper, or under 5% share | holder share (red on its own), otherwise the report |
| **Liquidity** (1.5) | Slippage | what a $10K buy costs (the rule of Degen gate 6) | under 1% on the DEX pool | 1–3% on the pool, or a thin pool with $2M or more traded on exchanges in 24 h | over 3% and under $2M a day | **live today** |
| | Market cap | today's "Micro" flag | over $20M | $3–20M | under $3M | **live today** |

| **Heat** (1) | Price run | 30-day change, distance to ATH | under +30% | +30–70% | over +70%, or +53% or more within 10% of the ATH | **live today** |

| | Audit | is the deployed code audited | yes | sister code only | none listed | report (starts from DefiLlama's audit list) |

Seven checks already exist on the live page in some form, seven are new and fully automatic, four wait for the report. Reaches you is already red from data when the holder share is under 5% (today's "No holder share" flag); otherwise it waits. Audit stays "not checked" until the report, which starts from DefiLlama's audit list and adds what the audits cover. Data for the automatic ones (contract owner, proxy admin, Safe threshold, timelock delay, holder lists, wallet age) is on-chain; public explorers and DeFiLlama (audits, hacks) cover most of it, paid feeds welcome. **A check that cannot be run is "not checked", never green.**

**Reading the table**
- **Cells are literal.** "Under X" and "over X" exclude X, "X or more" includes X, "A–B" includes both ends, "A to under B" includes A but not B. So a real yield of exactly 2× is green and an emission rate of exactly 10% is light blue. Decide the colour on the raw value, not on a computed score, so the edges are not left to rounding.
- **Slippage is Degen gate 6, not a second opinion.** Gate 6 ("a $10,000 buy without heavy slippage") passes a coin when the DEX pool moves 3% or less on $10,000, or when $2M or more traded on exchanges in the last 24 h. Slippage uses the same two numbers and the same limits, so a Degen pick can never show a red Slippage circle next to a gate it passed. Today 4 of the 9 Degen picks pass gate 6 on volume while their DEX pool would move 6% to 100% on $10,000 (Solv, Marinade, Lista DAO, Velodrome); they read light blue, not red. The circle's tooltip names its source: "DEX pool" when the pool decides, "exchange volume" when the volume rescues a thin pool. With no pool at all, $2M or more a day reads light blue, less is not checked.
- **Low float** is today's "float" flag, folded into Unlocks: a coin with only a few percent of its tokens out can keep unlocking for years, whatever the next 90 days say. If either input of Unlocks is missing, the check can turn light blue or red but never green.
- **Definitions.** *Float* is the circulating supply behind today's market cap. An *insider* is a wallet provably tied to the project, on-chain or in its own docs (the deployer, the owners of the admin Safe, the recipients of the founding allocation), never a guess; a tooltip says "inferred" when any doubt is left. Vesting unlocks belong to Unlocks and are not counted twice in Hidden supply.
- **Other chains.** Admin key and Admin powers have equivalents outside EVM (upgrade, mint and freeze authority); use them. A check that does not exist for an app (for example a timelock on a chain without one) is **n/a**: grey with a solid outline, left out of its area, and it does not widen the range. An area in which every check is n/a is left out, and the weights of the other areas are rescaled to add up to 10.
- **Data quality** (the job of today's "New" and "Stale data" flags). With under six months of data, Trend, Price run and Strength 3M are "not checked: under six months of data". If the latest data point is over 10 days old, Real yield, Trend, Slippage, Market cap, Price run and Strength 3M are "not checked: stale data". Both widen the range like any missing check, and the level strip says why.

**Our proposed arithmetic** (yours to change; the reference implements it under "Behind the scenes"):
- each check → a risk from 0 to 1 (green up to 0.25, light blue 0.25–0.6, red from 0.6; not checked counts as 0.5);
- an area = 60% of its worst check + 40% of the average of its checks;
- level = the sum of weight × area, rounded, at least 1 (the weights of the areas that apply add up to 10);
- floors: two facts from the contract scan give at least 8 — the source of the main token or staking contract is not verified, or an EOA can mint without a cap. They are not checks of their own; the Admin powers tooltip names them;
- **range:** run it twice, with every "not checked" as best case and as worst case. If the two levels differ, the meter outlines the segments up to the higher one with dashes and the detail says "Range 8–9 · 3 checks need the report". The report removes most of the range. **When the report fills in the missing checks, the level may move, but for the same data it stays inside the range shown before the report: that is what the range promises.** It leaves that range only when the data itself changed (for example the admin key was replaced), and then the level strip says "Was 8 at the last report".
- **Before it goes live,** run the level on every app and put the distribution in your note: how many apps at each level, how many with a range wider than two levels. If more than a third are that uncertain, tell us; we would rather pre-generate reports for the top apps than show a wall of dashed meters.

### D. Colour rule

Green = good for a buyer, **light blue = caution** (as in today's Legend: amber is off Cymetica's palette), red = bad, grey or dashed = missing. **The Upside pill and tile are the exception: they keep today's live tier colours (A.8), where grey means little upside, not missing.** These colours judge one coin for a buyer of that coin; the Altseason panel (part 1) colours its words for the market. They share a palette, not a meaning. The same number has the same colour in the row, the tiles, the table and the charts. **Do not use the brand accent for anything that carries a verdict**: it is close to light blue. Charts without a verdict (Monthly revenue) may use it. **The Chart line, its number and the Chart card use the analyzer's bands** (green 61–100, light blue 41–60, red 1–40; see F). They colour a reading of a chart, not a verdict on the coin, so a green line can sit next to a red Degen level. Use your own palette and fonts; keep the look: dark, big readable type (body text 16 px or more, key numbers 28 px or more), few words.

### F. The Chart column and the Chart card (Chart Analyzer)

Cymetica's Chart Analyzer (`https://cymetica.com/chart-analyzer`) reads a chart as price and volume candles, finds chart patterns on it and scores it from 1 (very bearish) to 100 (very bullish) from success rates it measured on real candles. Use it as it is; do not build a second one.

- **Data.** `GET /api/v1/chart-analyzer/analyze?symbol=<ticker>&timeframe=1d` (or `1w`) returns `score`, `band` (very bearish, bearish, neutral, bullish, very bullish: the 20-point buckets 1–20, 21–40, 41–60, 61–80, 81–100 of the analyzer's own study), `candles_used`, `patterns`, `as_of` and `last_candle_forming`; add `&include_candles=true` and it also returns the 500 candles the score was computed on (`[time, open, high, low, close, volume]`, about 40 kB per coin). Colour by the returned `band`: bullish and very bullish green, neutral light blue, bearish and very bearish red. Do not recompute the thresholds.
- **Coverage (measured on 2026-09-29).** The analyzer reads only Binance USDT pairs. The chart is for the Apps tab only (chains keep today's detail). Of the 210 distinct app tickers, 84 have a daily score (40%); 68 of those 84 also have a weekly score, and the other 16 have fewer than 60 weekly candles. All the rest show the dashed "no score" state. Match a coin to its Binance pair by its **CoinGecko id, never by ticker alone** (tickers collide). You may also send our own candles for coins without a pair (`POST /api/v1/chart-analyzer/analyze` with `candles`), but the analyzer's rates were measured on Binance daily candles only; if you do, mark the score "from our candles". Not required.
- **The line.** Draw the closes of the same candles the score was computed on: the last 90 daily or 52 weekly closes. Read them from the analyzer's own response (`include_candles=true`, above), or from a compact closes option if the analyzer has one by then; do not fetch a second series from somewhere else.
- **Refresh and closed candles.** The analyzer's default includes the candle that is still open (`last_candle_forming`), so a colour can flip between two refreshes without a candle closing: on 2026-09-29, 77 of the 84 daily app scores used an open candle. **Use the last closed candle for the row and the card:** drop the open candle from the candles you read and score the rest (the analyzer also scores candles you send: `POST /api/v1/chart-analyzer/analyze` with `candles`), or ask it for a closed-only option, and say in your note which you did. Refresh once after each daily close (UTC) and once after each weekly close, in the background, never per visitor.
- **Calls and the limit.** The analyzer allows 30 requests per window (the `x-ratelimit-limit` header; the window is about a minute, see `x-ratelimit-reset`). A refresh needs one call per covered app and timeframe: about 150 (84 daily and 68 weekly), or about 300 if you also score the closed candles yourself with a second call. Coins without a pair are probed again once a week (126 calls), not on every refresh. Stay under the limit by pacing the calls (about 25 a minute, so a refresh takes 6 to 12 minutes, which is fine at one refresh a day) or with a batch call or a higher limit if the analyzer offers one; the row shows the last finished refresh. Say in your note what you chose.
- **Daily and weekly can disagree.** Of the 68 apps with both, 35 sit in a different band. That is why the row says Daily and the card has the switch.
- **A reading, not advice, and display only.** The analyzer's own test on data it did not learn from (2023-01-01 to 2026-09-29) found no reliable edge: charts scoring 81–100 rose 10 candles later in 45.6% of 2,208 cases, against 44.4% for all candles; rank correlation −0.02. That is why the card carries its "Not a forecast" line, and why the Chart score is never a sort key, a filter, a gate, a part of the Degen level, or an input to Top Picks, Degen picks or any fund. The colour says what the chart looks like, not that the coin is good: a coin can be Degen level 8 and bullish at the same time. Keep the sentence true to the analyzer's published study (`GET /api/v1/chart-analyzer/study`): if its result changes, so does the sentence.
- **Before it goes live,** put the distribution of daily scores over all covered apps in your note. On 2026-09-29 the 84 covered apps were 48 bullish, 31 neutral, 3 very bullish and 2 bearish (51 green, 61%), so the column will look green-heavy; that is the analyzer's scale, not a bug.
